Our specialties

Stroke Rehabilitation

We are dedicated to helping stroke survivors regain their independence and improve their quality of life. Our state-of-the-art facility and experienced team of healthcare professionals are here to support you on your journey to recovery.

Knee Rehabilitation

At Rebound, we are dedicated to helping individuals recover from knee injuries and surgeries, regain mobility, and live pain-free. Our specialized Knee Rehabilitation Center is designed to provide comprehensive care and support for knee-related conditions.

Spinal Cord injury Rehabilitation

We are committed to helping individuals with spinal cord injuries regain their independence, mobility, and quality of life. Our specialized facility and dedicated team of professionals are here to support you on your journey to recovery.

Paediatric Rehabilitation

We are dedicated to the well-being and development of children. Our specialized Pediatric Rehabilitation Center is committed to helping children overcome challenges and reach their full potential. We provide expert care in a supportive and child-friendly environment.
